## E90 Desk Toy

Another simplification of an existing design, I produced several dozen of these desk toys early into my time with Xylem. Of the ~23,000 employees at Xylem spread across 150 countries, I was the first employee to ever hold the title "Additive Manufacturing Design Engineer".

As a result of that and my seat within New Product Development one of my early objectives was to find a fun and cost-effective way to get a 110-year-old manufacturing facility excited about the deployment of the new technology I was representing. This desk toy came out of that.

My (former) name was circulated widely, and before too long a healthy queue of projects and ideas started organically shuffling their way to my desk.
